# Common Error Messages and Resolutions

## Frequently Asked Questions About Errors

This page documents common error symptoms and their resolution steps.

### "Account Not Found"

This error appears on the login screen when no account exists for the entered email address. Resolution: check for a typo in your email address. If correct, you may need to create a new account or contact support to locate your account credentials.

### "Session Expired"

Your login session expires after a period of inactivity. Resolution: log out completely and log back in with your credentials. Enable "Remember Me" to extend session duration.

### "Subscription Required"

This error appears when you try to access a premium feature without an active subscription. Resolution: upgrade your subscription in Settings > Billing. If you already have a subscription, check that your invoice payment was processed successfully.

### "File Not Found" During Upload

Resolution: verify that the file path is correct and the file has not been moved. Check that the file size does not exceed the upload limit. Try uploading again after a browser refresh.

### "Network Connection Failed"

Resolution: check your internet connection. If using a VPN or proxy, try disabling it temporarily. Clear your browser cache and cookies and retry.

### "Permission Denied"

Resolution: your account may not have the required permissions. Contact your account administrator to request access. If you are the administrator, check that your subscription includes the requested feature.

### "Activation Code Invalid or Expired"

Resolution: request a new activation email. Activation codes expire after 48 hours. If the issue persists, check that you are entering the code for the correct account.
