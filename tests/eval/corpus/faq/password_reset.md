# Password Reset Troubleshoot

## How to Reset Your Password

If you cannot login to your account, follow these troubleshoot steps to reset your credentials.

### Step 1: Request Password Reset

Visit the login page and click "Forgot Password". Enter your account email address. A password reset link will be sent to your inbox within a few minutes.

### Step 2: Check Your Inbox

If you do not receive the reset email, check your spam folder. The reset link expires after 24 hours. Request a new link if your previous one has expired.

### Step 3: Set New Credentials

Click the reset link and enter a new password. Passwords must be at least 8 characters and include letters and numbers. Confirm the new password and click "Save".

### Account Locked Troubleshoot

If your account is locked after too many failed login attempts, wait 30 minutes and try again. For immediate unlock, contact support with your account credentials and identity verification.

### Common Password Problems

- **Forgot password**: Use the reset link above.
- **Password not accepted**: Check that Caps Lock is off and try again.
- **Reset link expired**: Request a new reset link from the login page.
- **Account suspended**: Contact billing support to check your subscription status.
