# Billing and Subscription FAQ

## Common Billing Questions

### How do I view my invoice?

Log into your account and navigate to Settings > Billing. All invoices and receipts are listed with date, amount, and payment status. Click any invoice to download a PDF copy.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

To cancel your subscription, go to Settings > Subscription and click "Cancel Plan". Your subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period. No refund is issued for partial months.

### How do I request a refund?

Refund requests must be submitted within 14 days of the billing date. Contact support with your account email and invoice number. Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days.

### Subscription Expiry and Renewal

Your subscription renews automatically on the billing anniversary date. You will receive a renewal reminder email 7 days before expiry. To prevent automatic renewal, disable auto-renew in Settings > Subscription.

### Incorrect Invoice Amount

If your invoice shows the wrong amount, check whether a promo code was applied correctly. If the error persists, contact billing support with your account details and invoice ID.

### Payment Method Update

To update your credit card or payment method, go to Settings > Billing > Payment Methods. Old payment methods are removed after you add a replacement.

### Subscription Upgrade and Downgrade

You can upgrade your subscription at any time. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. Prorated credits are applied when upgrading mid-cycle.
