# License Activation FAQ

## How to Activate Your License

After purchasing a subscription, you will receive a license key by email. Enter the key in the application under Settings > License > Activate.

### Invalid License Key

If the activation fails with "invalid key", check for typos. License keys are case-sensitive and include hyphens. Copy and paste the key directly from your email to avoid errors.

### License Expiry

Your license expires at the end of your subscription period. When expired, the application runs in limited mode. Renew your subscription to receive a new license key and reactivate.

### License Already in Use

Each license key is tied to a single account. If you see "license already in use", it may be activated on another device. Deactivate the previous device in Settings > License > Manage Devices before activating on a new one.

### Product Registration and Serial Number

Some products require product registration in addition to license activation. Register your serial number at the vendor portal using your account credentials. This links your purchase to your account profile for future reinstalls.

### License Transfer

To transfer a license to a new computer: deactivate on the old machine via Settings > License > Deactivate, then activate on the new machine using the same key.

### Enterprise License Pooling

Enterprise subscriptions include floating licenses. The license server manages allocation. Contact your IT administrator for license pool configuration and troubleshoot guides.
